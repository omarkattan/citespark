UAE AI Visibility Index → Property Developers

Named by AI, and almost never cited.

Twenty-two UAE property developers, put to Google AI Mode, Google AI Overview and ChatGPT with the questions buyers actually ask. We record whether a developer is named in the answer, where it appears, whether the answer recommends it, and whether its own website is cited as a source.

Scores compare developers within a cohort, never across cohorts. A Sharjah developer and a UAE major are answering different questions from different prompt sets. Placing one against the other would rank the questions, not the companies.

Method

How this is measured.

The score

A composite of four machine-verifiable components: 35% mention rate, 25% top-three rate, 20% recommendation rate, 20% citation rate. Every one can be recomputed from the stored answers.

What is not in the score

Accuracy, sentiment, hallucination rate and attribute association are human-annotated. We cannot reproduce them monthly across twenty-two developers, so we cannot defend them, so they do not enter the composite. They are published only where an audit has actually been done.

Runs and spread

Every question is asked three times per engine, because these models are not deterministic. We publish the mean and the range. A developer at 40% from runs of 20, 40 and 60 is a different finding from one at 38, 40 and 42.

Denominators

Rates are over answers that were returned, not attempts. Google shows an AI Overview for only some of these questions; counting the silent ones as failures to appear would report a gap in our measurement as an absence of the developer.

Companies, not projects

Naming Al Zahia is not naming Sharjah Holding. Project mentions are recorded separately and do not enter the corporate score. Where a developer has project mentions excluded, the count is shown against it.

Coverage

Google AI Mode, Google AI Overview and ChatGPT. Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Copilot are not covered in this release. Where a developer is invisible here, that is three surfaces, not the whole market.

What we withheld

One question asked which developers attract the most buyer complaints. We ran it and did not publish it. A ranked reputational claim about named companies, drawn from unverified model output, is not something we are willing to put on a public page.

Corrections

If a developer is missing, a domain is wrong, or a figure looks off, write to omar@sandstormdigital.com. The questions behind each cohort and the raw answers are retained, so any number here can be traced back to the answer that produced it.
